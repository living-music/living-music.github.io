# Living Music icon

## Concept

The Living Music icon combines a musical note with a leaf in one continuous silhouette. The note communicates playback immediately; the leaf gives “Living” a distinct meaning without adding text or a second competing symbol.

A deep forest-green field keeps the icon calm in dark-mode contexts. The mint and sage mark supplies enough contrast to remain identifiable at favicon and home-screen sizes. Soft depth helps it sit naturally beside current Apple-platform icons while the shape and palette remain original to Living Music.

The source artwork is full bleed and square. Do not add rounded corners; Apple platforms apply their own icon mask. Do not place text, a border, or a second logo over the mark.

## Color direction

- Background: deep forest and emerald green
- Mark: luminous mint through soft sage
- Interface accent: fresh green derived from the icon
- Default surrounding appearance: near-black dark mode

Exact sampled colors may be added when the broader design-token system is implemented.

## Generation prompt

The icon was generated with the built-in image-generation tool using this production brief:

> Design a simple, minimal green-themed app icon for Living Music. Center one bold musical note whose upper stroke subtly becomes a fresh leaf. Use a premium Apple-platform app-icon aesthetic, clean geometric silhouette, refined soft depth, a deep forest and emerald background, and a luminous mint-to-sage mark. Keep it legible at 32 px and full bleed for an operating-system mask. Include no text, border, mockup, staff lines, headphones, religious symbols, watermark, or busy texture.

Review the icon at 32, 60, 120, and 180 px before changing its silhouette or contrast.
